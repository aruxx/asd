# Executive Definitions

## Precision Buying Algorithm
Algorithmic programs activating when SMA outfit harmonics confirm statistically validated OHLC relationships. They magnetize liquidity during drawdowns and redeploy capital at pre-authorized price coordinates.

## Automated Short Order
Borrowed-share executions triggered when SMA structures forecast downward pivots. These run on millisecond event loops and rely on synchronized arbitrage detection plus dark pool routing.

## Singular Point Hard Stop Order
An enforced liquidation level set to a single tick or penny. These stops maintain deterministic exits for both precision longs and automated shorts, ensuring that massive order books can unwind without manual intervention.

## Optimized Buying Algorithm
Adaptive programs tuned for volatility regimes. Unlike precision buys (which act at one coordinate), optimized buys adjust parameters mid-flight to keep execution inside SMA-defined corridors even as liquidity thins.

## Magnetized Buying Program
Order-stacking protocols that attract resting liquidity to SMA bands. They emphasize minimal slippage and rely on overlapping OHLC counts relative to a single outfit.

## Dark Pool Coordination
Private routes that hide institutional participation while enforcing singular-point order precision. Their presence explains why public prints often lag real decision points.

## Institutional Hour Session
9:30 AM – 4:00 PM EST. Liquidity depth and SMA response are maximal here. Extended hours operate under variant configurations with lower volume but identical outfit logic.

These definitions unify the vocabulary used across UI copy, backend tooling, and documentation to eliminate ambiguity for contributors.
