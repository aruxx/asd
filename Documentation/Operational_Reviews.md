# Operational Reviews

Operational reviews are recurring assessments of SMA-driven strategies. Each review should capture:

- **Evaluation Window:** Dates, sessions, and market regimes covered.
- **Program Health:** Status of Precision Buying, Automated Short, and Optimized Buying algorithms, including uptime and exception logs.
- **Wealth Transfer Analysis:** Quantitative summary of capital redistribution attributed to SMA outfits.
- **Risk & Compliance Notes:** Observed deviations, regulatory disclosures, and remediation steps.

Use these reviews to compare expected versus realized behavior, ensuring the public documentation stays synchronized with live market conduct. EOF
