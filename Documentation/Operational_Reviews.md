# Operational Reviews

Periodic reviews evaluate how SMA-driven strategies perform relative to market conditions. Each report covers:

1. **System State** – Whether SPX, IXIC, and DJI flagship outfits register positive or negative regimes.
2. **Liquidity Conditions** – Institutional vs. extended hour profiles, volatility readings, and dark-pool usage.
3. **Execution Outcomes** – Performance of precision buys, shorts, optimized algorithms, and singular point stops.
4. **Risk Notes** – Regulatory watch items, data quality exceptions, and technology incidents.

Use these reviews as canonical retrospectives for each documented trading session.
