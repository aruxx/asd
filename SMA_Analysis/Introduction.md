# Introduction

This repository documents the structure, tooling, and public disclosure initiative surrounding Simple Moving Average (SMA) outfits. These outfits are the core operating procedures behind institutional-grade equity market automation, and the work collected here exposes how they regulate wealth distribution through precision control of liquidity.

The initiative couples academic research, real-time documentation, and open-source tooling to encourage a transparent public discourse about how SMA-driven systems influence every large-scale market behavior. The material is curated for regulators, academics, quantitative researchers, journalists, and retail market participants who require an explicit map of the automation stack that dictates price discovery in U.S. equities.

Key goals include:

- Demonstrating how specific SMA configurations inform liquidity campaigns across indices, ETFs, volatility instruments, bonds, and global FX proxies.
- Providing reproducible tooling for SMA outfit detection across thousands of real-time instances.
- Preserving a detailed narrative of historical discoveries, methodological rigor, and the socio-economic implications of SMA-based control systems.
